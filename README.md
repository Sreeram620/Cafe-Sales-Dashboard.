# ☕ Cafe Sales Analysis Dashboard

## 1. Project Overview

**Cafe Sales Analysis** is an interactive Power BI dashboard designed to analyze cafe sales performance across different locations, products, payment methods, and transactions.

The dashboard helps identify **top-selling products, revenue-generating products, payment-method performance, transaction volume, and location-based sales patterns** to support data-driven business decisions.

---

## 2. Project Description

The **Cafe Sales Dashboard** is an analytical Power BI report developed to explore sales and revenue performance.

The dashboard mainly focuses on:

* Sales and revenue performance
* Product sales and revenue
* Payment method analysis
* Transaction volume
* Location-based sales
* Identification of top-performing products

The objective is to transform raw cafe transaction data into meaningful business insights that can help improve **product availability, sales performance, and business decision-making**.

---

## 3. Tech Stack

The dashboard was developed using the following tools and technologies:

* **Power BI Desktop** – Used to build the interactive dashboard and visualizations
* **Python** – Used for data cleaning and preprocessing
* **Pandas & NumPy** – Used for data manipulation and cleaning

## 4. Data Source

**Source:** Kaggle

The dataset contains approximately **10,000 cafe sales transactions** with the following important fields:

* Transaction ID
* Item
* Quantity
* Price per Unit
* Total Spent
* Payment Method
* Location
* Transaction Date

The raw dataset required data cleaning and preprocessing before being used for analysis and visualization.

## 5. Dashboard Features & Highlights

### Business Problem

The cafe wants to understand its sales performance and identify opportunities to improve revenue and product sales.

### Goal of the Dashboard

The main goal is to analyze:

* Which products are selling the most?
* Which products generate the highest revenue?
* Which payment methods contribute the most revenue?
* How many transactions are being completed?
* Which locations contribute to sales?
* Where can the cafe improve its sales strategy?

### Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

The dashboard includes KPI cards for:

* **Total Revenue**
* **Total Quantity Sold**
* **Total Transactions**
* **Average Revenue per Transaction**

### Visualizations

#### 1. Revenue by Payment Method

A donut chart shows the revenue contribution from different payment methods such as:

* Digital Wallet
* Credit Card
* Cash

This helps understand customer payment preferences and their contribution to revenue.

#### 2. Quantity Sold by Product

A pie chart shows the quantity of products sold and helps identify the products with the highest sales volume.

#### 3. Revenue by Product

A bar chart compares the revenue generated by different products and highlights the highest revenue-generating products.

## 6. Business Insights

### Insight 1 – Missing Location Data

Approximately **40% of the location data was missing or unavailable** in the original dataset.

This limits the reliability of location-based analysis and makes it difficult to accurately determine which locations generate the highest sales.

**Business implication:** Location data should be captured accurately in future transactions to support reliable location-based decision-making.

### Insight 2 – Salad Revenue Performance

**Salad was identified as one of the highest revenue-generating products in the analysis.**

This indicates strong revenue contribution from the product.

**Business implication:** The cafe can monitor Salad's demand and ensure sufficient stock availability to reduce the possibility of stockouts and missed sales opportunities.

## 7. Data Cleaning

The raw dataset contained several data-quality issues that were handled during preprocessing using Python.

The cleaning process included:

* Handling missing values
* Handling invalid values such as `UNKNOWN` and `ERROR`
* Converting transaction dates into the appropriate date format
* Handling missing transaction dates
* Validating numerical columns
* Checking quantity and price values
* Creating/validating calculated sales values
* Preparing the cleaned dataset for Power BI analysis
